# Class 6 Structured Summary Template

Use this Markdown template when students need a clean summary handoff before the human review packet.

- Document type: service agreement / contract, privacy policy, or terms of service
- Overall risk: low / medium / high
- Human review required: yes / no
- Generated at: ISO timestamp or classroom run time

## Summary

Write a short first-pass explanation of what the document section does and why it matters.

## Key findings

- Finding 1: clause, risk level, and why it matters
- Finding 2: clause, risk level, and why it matters
- Finding 3: clause, risk level, and why it matters

## Missing information

- Unknown definitions, dates, fees, retention periods, or obligations

## Next steps

- Confirm unclear business facts
- Escalate high-risk clauses to human review
- Do not treat this as final legal advice

## Disclaimer

This is first-pass review support, not final legal advice.
