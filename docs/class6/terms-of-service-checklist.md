# Terms of Service Checklist

Use this checklist for a first-pass review of website or product terms of service. Route business-critical or unclear issues to human review.

## Core sections to verify

```text
[ ] acceptable use rules are stated clearly.
[ ] payment/refunds language matches the real business policy.
[ ] account or user obligations are specific and enforceable.
[ ] subscription/renewal rules disclose timing and cancellation.
[ ] suspension/termination terms are not unfairly one-sided.
[ ] liability limitation and disclaimers are visible.
[ ] update/notice process explains how users learn about changes.
```

## Common high-risk signals

- automatic renewal with weak notice
- non-refundable fees with no nuance
- sole-discretion suspension rights
- vague user obligations that are hard to enforce consistently
- missing update notice process

## Human review gate

If the language affects billing commitments, account access, dispute rights, or user-facing promises, send it to human review.
