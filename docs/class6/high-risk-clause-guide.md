# High-Risk Clause Guide

Use this guide to explain why a clause deserves escalation during Class 6 review.

## Categories that should trigger caution

- liability — uncapped exposure, broad damages, or one-sided risk transfer
- indemnity — open-ended duties to defend or reimburse another party
- privacy/data handling — unclear collection, sharing, retention, or transfer promises
- IP ownership/transfer — broad assignment of code, content, or deliverables
- termination/cancellation — one-sided exit rights or missing cure periods
- governing law/dispute resolution — hidden venue, arbitration, or forum surprises

## How to label the issue

```text
1. Quote the exact clause.
2. State the category.
3. Explain the practical business risk.
4. Note what facts are missing.
5. Recommend human review.
```

## Classroom reminder

This guide supports first-pass review support only. It does not replace legal advice or final legal approval.
