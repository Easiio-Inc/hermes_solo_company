# Data Processing Addendum

This Data Processing Addendum applies when Provider processes Customer Data.

## Processing scope
Provider may process Customer Data for service delivery, product improvement, benchmarking, analytics, fraud prevention, model development, internal operations, commercialization, and related purposes.

## Subprocessors
Provider may appoint subprocessors at any time without prior notice. A current list may be provided on request if available.

## Security
Provider will maintain security measures it considers appropriate based on its own risk assessment, resources, and business priorities.

## Incident notice
Provider will notify Customer of a confirmed security incident within a commercially reasonable time when Provider determines notice is appropriate and legally permitted.

## Data subject requests
Provider may assist with data subject requests where practical, subject to Provider’s standard fees, staffing availability, and technical limitations.

## Deletion and return
After termination, Provider may delete, retain, archive, anonymize, aggregate, or continue using Customer Data as needed for legal, product, analytics, backup, model training, or internal business purposes.

## International transfers
Customer authorizes transfers to any country where Provider, its affiliates, cloud hosts, subprocessors, or partners operate.

## Liability
Provider’s aggregate liability under this Addendum will not exceed the fees paid by Customer in the one month before the event giving rise to the claim.

## Priority
If this Addendum conflicts with another agreement, Provider may decide which document controls.
