# Independent Contractor Agreement

This Agreement is between ClientCo and Contractor.

## Services
Contractor will provide services as requested by ClientCo from time to time. Specific deliverables, deadlines, acceptance criteria, and revision limits may be communicated informally by message, call, or verbal request.

## Payment
ClientCo will pay Contractor at a rate to be determined later or as otherwise discussed. ClientCo may withhold payment for any reason it considers commercially reasonable, including dissatisfaction, budget changes, internal policy, or delayed customer payments.

## Expenses
Contractor is responsible for all tools, software, hardware, workspace, travel, taxes, insurance, assistants, and other expenses unless ClientCo chooses otherwise.

## Ownership
All work product, drafts, ideas, inventions, templates, know-how, methods, improvements, and anything related to the services shall belong exclusively to ClientCo from creation. Contractor also assigns to ClientCo any rights in pre-existing materials, reusable tools, and general know-how used while performing services.

## Confidentiality
Contractor must keep all non-public information strictly confidential forever.

## Non-compete / non-solicit
During the term of this Agreement and for five years after it ends, Contractor may not directly or indirectly work for, advise, invest in, or assist any business that ClientCo considers competitive anywhere in the world.

## Indemnity
Contractor will indemnify ClientCo and its affiliates against any claim, loss, damage, liability, cost, or expense arising from the services, whether or not caused in part by ClientCo.

## Liability
Contractor’s liability is unlimited. ClientCo has no liability to Contractor for any indirect, direct, special, incidental, consequential, or other damages.

## Termination
ClientCo may terminate this Agreement at any time immediately. Contractor must continue assisting with transition matters for as long as ClientCo requests. Contractor may not terminate the Agreement without ClientCo’s written consent.

## Disputes
Any dispute will be resolved under the laws of a jurisdiction selected by ClientCo after the dispute arises.
