# Service Agreement Checklist

Use this checklist for a first-pass review of a service agreement before moving any high-risk issue to human review.

## Core sections to verify

```text
[ ] scope of work is specific enough to deliver against.
[ ] payment terms clearly define amount, timing, and late-payment consequences.
[ ] refund/cancellation language explains what happens if the client stops early.
[ ] timeline/delivery terms define deadlines, approvals, and acceptance points.
[ ] IP ownership explains who owns work product, tools, and pre-existing materials.
[ ] confidentiality obligations are mutual or intentionally one-sided with business justification.
[ ] termination terms explain how either party can exit.
[ ] liability limitation is present and commercially reasonable.
[ ] dispute/governing law terms are visible and not surprising.
```

## Common high-risk signals

- unlimited liability
- broad indemnity with no cap
- client can withhold payment without a defined process
- one-sided termination with no cure period
- broad IP transfer beyond the paid deliverable

## Human review gate

If any clause changes liability exposure, payment exposure, IP ownership, or termination rights in a material way, send it to human review.
