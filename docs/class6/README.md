# Class 6 Documents

This folder contains the classroom documents for **Class 6 — AI Legal & Compliance Agent**.

## Purpose

These materials support teaching, demo preparation, and implementation of the Class 6 workflow:
- reviewing contracts, privacy policies, and terms of service
- identifying risky clauses
- producing structured summaries
- escalating high-risk issues to human review
- supporting the student/admin website experience

These documents are teaching and demo materials, not final legal advice.

## Suggested demo order

A practical classroom demo order is:
1. open the slide deck / presenter guide
2. use `demo-inputs.md` or `demo-inputs-zh.md`
3. review one of the files in `legal-review-demo-docs/`
4. compare findings against the relevant checklist
5. produce a structured summary and human-review escalation packet
6. optionally compare against the company playbook materials
