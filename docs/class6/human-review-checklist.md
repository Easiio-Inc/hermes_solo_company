# Class 6 Human Review Checklist

Use this when the AI flags a clause as high-risk or uncertain.

## Human review packet

```text
[ ] Copy the exact clause text.
[ ] Record the document type.
[ ] Note the AI risk label.
[ ] Explain why the clause may be risky.
[ ] List unknown facts or ambiguous wording.
[ ] Decide who should review it next.
```

## Escalation rules

- If a clause changes liability, indemnity, payment exposure, privacy obligations, or dispute rights, escalate to a lawyer.
- If the company is unsure whether a promise can be operationally met, escalate to the founder/operator as well.
- If the wording affects personal data handling, retention, or sharing, escalate to a privacy/compliance reviewer.

## Required reminder

This is not final legal advice.
The AI output helps organize review, but a human must approve high-risk decisions.
If the business impact is material, escalate to a lawyer before signing, publishing, or enforcing the clause.
