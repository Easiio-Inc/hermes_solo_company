# AI Solo Company Class 6 — 26-Slide PPT Deck

**Class title:** AI Legal & Compliance Agent  
**Chinese title:** 第 6 课：AI 法律与合规 Agent  
**Deck goal:** Turn the Class 6 lesson into a clear 26-slide teaching deck with one clean teaching slice per slide.  
**Core boundary:** This is first-pass review support, not final legal advice.

---

## Slide 1 — Title
**Title:** Class 6: AI Legal & Compliance Agent  
**Subtitle:** Review contracts, privacy policies, and terms of service with AI — then escalate high-risk issues to human review.  
**Visual:** Document → Checklist → Risk Flags → Human Review → Compliance Summary

## Slide 2 — Where Class 6 fits in the bootcamp
**Title:** Where this class fits  
**Key points:**
- Earlier: website, lead capture, skills, finance workflows
- Now: legal/compliance review workflow
- Later: operations, sales, support, growth systems
**Speaker note:** This class adds risk control to the company OS.

## Slide 3 — The founder problem
**Title:** Founders sign risky things all the time  
**Key points:**
- Client agreements
- Contractor agreements
- Privacy policies
- Terms of service
- Refund/cancellation language
- Data disclosures
**Speaker note:** Most founders are not sure what is missing, risky, or lawyer-worthy.

## Slide 4 — Why this matters for AI solo companies
**Title:** Fast growth without compliance creates hidden risk  
**Key points:**
- Revenue terms can be unclear
- Liability can be one-sided
- Privacy promises can be incomplete
- Website policies can conflict with actual operations
**Speaker note:** Automation is dangerous if the legal layer is weak.

## Slide 5 — What this agent is
**Title:** What an AI legal/compliance agent should do  
**Key points:**
- Run checklist review
- Scan clauses for obvious risk
- Detect missing sections
- Summarize issues clearly
- Prepare human review packets

## Slide 6 — What this agent is not
**Title:** What this agent should never claim  
**Key points:**
- Not a lawyer
- Not final legal advice
- Not a signoff authority
- Not a jurisdiction-specific expert by default
**Speaker note:** The agent supports review; humans approve high-risk decisions.

## Slide 7 — Today’s class outcome
**Title:** Deliverable for today  
**Key points:**
- Build a compliance checking workflow
- Review one sample document
- Flag risks by severity
- Escalate high-risk items to human review
- Produce a structured review summary
**Output label:** 合规检查流程

## Slide 8 — The core workflow
**Title:** The Class 6 workflow  
**Flow:**
- Document intake
- Document type detection
- Checklist selection
- Clause scan
- Risk flagging
- Missing-section detection
- Human review escalation
- Compliance summary

## Slide 9 — The three document families
**Title:** Three core document types  
**Columns:**
1. Contracts / agreements
2. Privacy policy
3. Terms of service
**Examples:**
- service agreement
- contractor agreement
- website privacy policy
- subscription terms

## Slide 10 — Document type 1: contracts
**Title:** What we review in contracts  
**Checklist items:**
- Scope of work
- Payment terms
- Cancellation / termination
- IP ownership
- Confidentiality
- Liability limits
- Dispute / governing law

## Slide 11 — Document type 2: privacy policy
**Title:** What we review in privacy policies  
**Checklist items:**
- What data is collected
- Why it is collected
- Cookies / tracking
- Sharing with third parties
- User rights / contact method
- Retention / deletion
- Security statements

## Slide 12 — Document type 3: terms of service
**Title:** What we review in terms of service  
**Checklist items:**
- Acceptable use
- Subscription / renewal rules
- Refund policy
- Suspension / termination
- Liability limitation
- Dispute resolution
- Update / notice process

## Slide 13 — The checklist mindset
**Title:** Why checklist review matters  
**Key points:**
- It reduces random prompting
- It forces consistency
- It helps compare documents
- It exposes missing sections quickly
**Speaker note:** Good compliance review starts with structure, not guesswork.

## Slide 14 — Risk model overview
**Title:** Our 3-level risk rubric  
**Levels:**
- Low risk
- Medium risk
- High risk
**Speaker note:** Simple beats fancy for classroom use.

## Slide 15 — Low risk examples
**Title:** What low risk looks like  
**Examples:**
- Minor wording cleanup
- Formatting issues
- Small missing detail
- Clarification with low business impact

## Slide 16 — Medium risk examples
**Title:** What medium risk looks like  
**Examples:**
- Ambiguous payment timing
- Weak refund wording
- Unclear ownership language
- Incomplete privacy disclosures

## Slide 17 — High risk examples
**Title:** What high risk looks like  
**Examples:**
- Unlimited liability
- One-sided indemnity
- Broad IP transfer
- Missing data/privacy disclosures
- Unilateral suspension rights
- Missing dispute structure where needed

## Slide 18 — The human review gate
**Title:** When AI must escalate  
**Decision rule:**
- If high risk → human review
- If liability, privacy, IP, or dispute rights are affected → human review
- If wording is only minor/clarity issue → revise internally first
**Reminder:** AI review is a first-pass tool, not final legal advice.

## Slide 19 — Demo system view
**Title:** The Class 6 review workspace  
**Show:**
- document type selector
- pasted document text
- structured review button
- findings list
- human review queue
- JSON / structured output
**Speaker note:** We are turning review into a repeatable workflow.

## Slide 20 — Demo 1: service agreement
**Title:** Demo 1 — service agreement review  
**Sample issues to show:**
- Client may terminate at any time
- Liability is not limited
- Payment may be withheld
**Expected findings:**
- one-sided termination rights
- unlimited liability exposure
- payment ambiguity

## Slide 21 — Demo 2: privacy policy
**Title:** Demo 2 — privacy policy review  
**Sample issues to show:**
- broad data collection
- sharing with partners/service providers
- vague retention period
**Expected findings:**
- third-party data sharing
- indefinite or unclear retention
- missing user-rights clarity

## Slide 22 — Demo 3: terms of service
**Title:** Demo 3 — terms of service review  
**Sample issues to show:**
- auto-renewal
- non-refundable fees
- suspension at sole discretion
**Expected findings:**
- auto-renewal commitment
- rigid refund rule
- unilateral account suspension

## Slide 23 — What good output looks like
**Title:** Structured compliance output  
**Output fields:**
- Document type
- Summary
- Flagged clauses
- Risk level
- Missing sections / missing facts
- Human review required
- Next actions

## Slide 24 — Human review packet
**Title:** What to send to a human reviewer  
**Packet fields:**
- Exact clause text
- Document type
- AI risk label
- Why it may be risky
- Unknown facts / ambiguity
- Suggested next reviewer
**Speaker note:** Escalation quality matters as much as detection quality.

## Slide 25 — Student exercise
**Title:** Student exercise  
**Instructions:**
- Choose one document type
- Run the checklist review
- List top 3 risks
- Identify missing sections
- Mark human review items
- Write next actions

## Slide 26 — Final takeaway
**Title:** The point of Class 6  
**Closing message:**
- We are not building an AI lawyer
- We are building a repeatable compliance workflow
- AI helps detect and organize risk earlier
- Humans still approve high-risk decisions
**Final line:** Build the workflow first. Trust the workflow more than a one-off answer.

- Slides 1–4: opening + business context

### Visual guidance
- Use dark title and closing slides
- Use simple 3-column comparison slides for document types
- Use risk-color coding:
  - low = green
  - medium = amber
  - high = red
- Use icons for document, checklist, warning, human review, export
