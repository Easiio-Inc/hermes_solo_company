# Class 11 Market Research Agent

## Purpose
Use this Class 11 skill to help a student, founder, or instructor turn **raw market evidence** into one clear market opportunity recommendation.

The goal is to convert a market brief and a small set of evidence blocks into:
- pain-point clusters
- competitor gap analysis
- 3 to 5 opportunity ideas
- scored opportunities
- one recommended niche
- one practical next validation action

## Core principle
Do **not** make the first pass depend on live scraping.

The workflow should work reliably from:
- pasted reviews
- forum posts
- customer complaints
- competitor copy
- pricing notes
- interview notes
- support transcripts
- product comparison notes

## Input checklist
Collect or request:
- Business topic
- Target audience
- Geography if relevant
- Market goal
- Competitor names if known
- Product category or business model if known
- Founder background or constraints if useful
- 3 to 10 evidence blocks

## Workflow
1. define the market
2. normalize the evidence
3. run pain-point analysis
4. run competitor gap analysis
5. generate 3 to 5 opportunities
6. score the opportunities
7. return one recommended niche and next validation step

## Output format
## Market brief summary
## Evidence quality note
## Pain-point analysis
## Competitor gap analysis
## Opportunity shortlist
## Opportunity scoring
## Recommended niche
## Risks and assumptions
## Best next validation step
