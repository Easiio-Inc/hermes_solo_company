## Section 4 — How the AI Market Research Agent Works

### H2
How the Class 11 workflow works

### Step 1
**Define the market**  
Tell Hermes what market, audience, and niche you want to explore.

### Step 2
**Paste real evidence**  
Add reviews, Reddit/forum posts, customer complaints, competitor copy, pricing notes, or interview notes.

### Step 3
**Find pain points**  
Hermes groups repeated complaints into clear customer pain clusters.

### Step 4
**Compare competitors**  
Hermes shows what competitors do well, where they are weak, and what segment they may be missing.

### Step 5
**Generate opportunities**  
Hermes suggests practical product or service opportunities based on pain and market gaps.

### Step 6
**Rank the best niche**  
Hermes scores the ideas and recommends one best opportunity to test first.

### Step 7
**Export the final report**  
You leave with a structured market opportunity brief you can use in later classes.

## Section 14 — FAQ

### H2
Frequently Asked Questions

#### FAQ 1
**Do I need a lot of data before using this?**
No. You can start with a small amount of evidence, such as 3 to 5 useful snippets from reviews, forums, or competitor notes. More evidence usually improves quality, but you do not need a huge dataset to begin.

#### FAQ 2
**Does Hermes scrape the internet automatically?**
The MVP workflow is designed to work from evidence you provide directly. That makes the classroom demo more reliable and keeps the reasoning reviewable.

#### FAQ 3
**What kinds of businesses can this help with?**
It works well for SaaS ideas, productized services, workflow automation offers, and niche business opportunities where customer pain and competitor gaps can be described clearly.

#### FAQ 4
**Can this help me choose between several niches?**
Yes. Hermes can compare several opportunity ideas and rank them based on demand, competition, monetization potential, and launch feasibility.

#### FAQ 5
**What should I do after Class 11?**
Use your winning niche to move into offer design, landing page creation, SEO content, outreach, or a pilot validation plan.
