# Class 12 Slide Outline — AI Customer Service and Sales Agent

Use this outline to build the Class 12 deck, teacher-led walkthrough, or short student handout.

---

## Slide 1 — Title
**Class 12: AI Customer Service and Sales Agent**
- Turn one inquiry into a useful business action
- Review-first routing, follow-up, CRM handoff, and proposal prep

**Speaker note:**
Today is about operational judgment, not just prompting. Students should learn how to handle inbound demand like an owner or operator.

---

## Slide 2 — What students will learn
By the end of Class 12, students should be able to:
- classify an inquiry as service, sales, nurture, or weak fit
- score the lead from 1 to 5
- draft email, DM, and call follow-up
- prepare a CRM-ready handoff
- decide whether a lead is ready for proposal review

---

## Slide 3 — Why this class matters
Most AI demos stop at answering questions.
Real businesses need a workflow that decides:
- who deserves fast follow-up
- what to say next
- what goes into the CRM
- when to escalate toward a proposal

**Teaching point:**
The value is not only the chatbot answer. The value is the system behind the answer.

---

## Slide 4 — The Class 12 workflow
Show the end-to-end flow:
1. lead intake
2. route classification
3. lead scoring
4. response drafting
5. CRM handoff
6. queue review
7. proposal review
8. exportable handoff for future bot skills

---

## Slide 5 — Review-first principle
**Rule:** nothing is auto-sent in the teaching flow.
- operator reviews first
- approval gates stay visible
- CRM write is explicit
- proposal export is explicit

**Speaker note:**
Students should understand that safe AI workflows are often more useful than fully autonomous ones.

---

## Slide 6 — Route types
Teach the four routes:
- **sales_qualified** — clear pain, likely fit, good next-step opportunity
- **customer_service** — needs clarification/help, not strong buying intent yet
- **nurture** — possible fit later, needs more information or timing
- **weak_fit** — poor fit, research-only, or low-priority inquiry

---

## Slide 7 — Lead scoring model
Students evaluate:
- fit
- urgency
- budget signal
- timeline signal
- business pain / specificity

**Teaching point:**
A score is not magic. It is a structured judgment tool.

---

## Slide 8 — Response package
For strong leads, the workflow returns:
- best CTA
- email draft
- DM draft
- call script

**Teaching point:**
Each channel has different length and tone, but all should point to one clear next action.

---

## Slide 9 — CRM handoff
Show what a useful CRM handoff contains:
- lead summary
- stage recommendation
- follow-up task
- missing information
- operator next action

**Speaker note:**
If the result cannot be pasted into a CRM, it is not yet operational.

---

## Slide 10 — Queue and operator review
Explain the queue statuses:
- pending review
- approved
- deferred
- completed

Explain why the queue matters:
- triages multiple leads
- supports human review
- prevents premature outreach

---

## Slide 11 — Proposal review and escalation
Some leads deserve proposal prep.
Students should learn to ask:
- is the business pain clear?
- is the fit strong enough?
- is budget/timeline credible?
- do we know the decision-maker?

**Teaching point:**
Proposal work is expensive. Qualification protects time.

---

## Slide 12 — Structured export for future skill creation
Class 12 now supports two export types:
- markdown handoff for humans
- JSON handoff for future Hermes/bot ingestion

This helps students see how one reviewed workflow becomes a reusable website-operation skill.

---

## Slide 13 — Demo lead
Use one live example:
- Sarah Chen
- Smile Dental
- wants after-hours patient Q&A and appointment capture
- budget around $1500/month
- timeline this month

Ask students:
- route?
- score?
- next action?
- proposal-ready or not?

---

## Slide 14 — Common mistakes
- treating every inquiry like a hot sales lead
- skipping qualification
- writing generic AI hype
- forgetting CRM next action
- jumping to proposal too early

---

## Slide 15 — In-class exercise
Give students 3 to 4 leads and ask them to produce:
- route
- lead score
- best CTA
- one response draft
- CRM handoff summary

Optional extension:
- choose which one deserves proposal export

---

## Slide 16 — Homework / extension
Students can extend Class 12 by:
- adding more industry lead examples
- refining qualification rules
- writing better CRM summaries
- turning one exported handoff into a reusable website workflow skill
