# Class 12 Demo Case — AI Solo Company

Use this demo case to teach Class 12 with the AI Solo Company business context.

---

## Business context
AI Solo Company helps small business owners and solo founders use AI to automate lead handling, customer support, follow-up, and business operations.

## Example inquiry
```text
Name: Jason Lin
Company: local law office
Source: website chatbot
Inquiry: We are missing after-hours lead inquiries and want to know if you can set up an AI assistant to answer common questions and collect consultation requests.
Budget: around $1200/month
Timeline: this month
```

## Expected teaching output
Students should be able to produce:
- lead summary
- route classification: sales-qualified
- qualification score: 4 or 5 depending on assumptions
- consultative email draft
- short DM draft
- short call script
- CRM summary
- stage recommendation
- next action

## Teaching points
1. The lead has a real workflow pain.
2. The need is specific enough to qualify.
3. The next step should be narrow and practical.
4. The response should sound founder-to-founder, not like generic agency spam.

## Suggested next action
Recommend a short workflow review call focused on after-hours inquiry capture, intake qualification, and CRM follow-up routing.
