# Class 7 Brand Visual System Skill

## Trigger
Use this skill when a student or teacher needs to turn a business idea into a usable brand brief, visual direction, prompt pack, and website-ready design system for the AI Solo Company Class 7 lesson.

## Purpose
Create one chosen direction that can actually be applied to a website, not just a pile of aesthetic options.

## Inputs
- Business name
- Offer / product
- Target audience
- Desired tone
- Visual constraints
- Website CTA

## Workflow
1. Clarify the business promise and target audience.
2. Write a concise brand brief.
3. Generate three visual directions with different positioning angles.
4. Pick one direction and explain why it wins.
5. Build prompt-pack outputs for logo, hero image, social image, and website graphics.
6. Convert the chosen direction into a lightweight design system.
7. Map the design system into homepage hero, section cards, and CTA usage.

## Guardrails
- Treat outputs as creative direction, not final approved branding.
- Prefer one selected direction over endless exploration.
- Keep the visual system tied to conversion and website use.
- Redact any secrets or private materials as `[REDACTED]`.

## Outputs
- Brand brief
- Three visual directions
- Final chosen direction
- Prompt pack
- Design-system summary
- Website integration checklist
