# SEO Article Writer Skill

## Purpose
Use this student skill after the brief is approved when you need one complete Class 8 article draft for human review.

## Input checklist
- Business / company
- Topic
- Primary keyword
- Selected brief or outline
- CTA
- Proof points
- Optional FAQ ideas

## Output goals
1. Recap the article brief so the reader knows the assignment.
2. Write one complete draft in a practical, buyer-friendly tone.
3. Include a visible CTA and FAQ section.
4. Include an on-page SEO checklist for review.
5. End with a next step that leads into a WordPress-safe handoff, not direct publishing.

## Guardrails
- Keep the draft review-safe and classroom-friendly.
- Do not pretend the article was published.
- Avoid unsupported claims, fake testimonials, or fake metrics.
- Prefer a clear structure over keyword stuffing.

## Output format
## Article brief recap
## Full article draft
## On-page SEO checklist
## Next action
