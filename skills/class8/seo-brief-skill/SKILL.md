# SEO Brief Skill

## Purpose
Use this student skill after keyword research when you need to choose the strongest Class 8 content angle and prepare a structured SEO brief.

## Input checklist
- Business / company
- Offer
- Audience
- Region
- Topic
- Primary keyword
- Supporting keywords
- Optional proof points or differentiators

## Output goals
1. Restate the brief objective in simple business language.
2. Pick one selected brief angle instead of trying to write everything at once.
3. Provide an SEO title, meta description, and search intent.
4. Provide a practical outline with FAQ and internal link ideas.
5. End with a next step that leads into the article-writing workflow.

## Guardrails
- Keep the output review-safe and student-editable.
- Do not invent live rankings or traffic claims.
- Prefer one practical conversion goal and one reader intent.
- Keep the internal links generic unless real site evidence exists.

## Output format
## Brief objective
## Selected brief
## Outline
## Internal links
## Next action
