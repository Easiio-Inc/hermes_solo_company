# Class 14 30-Day Launch Plan

## Purpose
Use this Class 14 skill when a student needs a practical next-30-days execution plan after the final integration class.

The goal is to produce a realistic four-week plan that helps the student move from demo stage into real launch activity.

## Input checklist
Collect or infer when available:
- business model
- target customer
- current website readiness
- assistant readiness
- lead capture readiness
- CRM and follow-up readiness
- content / SEO / GEO readiness
- short-video or publishing readiness
- proposal / pricing readiness
- launch blockers
- available weekly capacity
- desired launch date

If key details are missing, mark them as assumptions and keep the plan conservative.

## 4-week structure
### Week 1 — Fix blockers
Focus on:
- website or landing page gaps
- FAQ or assistant knowledge gaps
- lead form or CRM gaps
- quote / proposal asset gaps

### Week 2 — Set up traffic and proof
Focus on:
- SEO or GEO content
- short-video or publishing channel
- FAQ or comparison pages
- proof assets such as case examples, trust sections, or testimonials

### Week 3 — Practice follow-up and conversion
Focus on:
- follow-up scripts
- consultation or inquiry flow
- quote / proposal sending process
- objection handling practice

### Week 4 — Launch and iterate
Focus on:
- first real outreach or publishing push
- lead response speed
- proposal / quote review
- early metrics review and improvement priorities

## Planning rules
- Keep the plan realistic for one operator.
- Prefer a narrow but executable plan over a broad ambitious plan.
- Tie each week to business outcomes, not just tasks.
- When possible, include success criteria for the week.

## Success metrics examples
Use or adapt metrics like:
- number of content pieces published
- number of inbound leads
- number of follow-ups sent
- number of consultations booked
- number of quotes or proposals sent
- first paying customer or first serious pipeline opportunities

- Do not make the plan depend on major new systems unless truly necessary.
- Prioritize actions that move the student toward real leads and real conversations.
