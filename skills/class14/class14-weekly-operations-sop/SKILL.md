# Class 14 Weekly Operations SOP

## Purpose
Use this Class 14 skill when a student needs a repeatable operating rhythm after the bootcamp.

The goal is to turn a working or almost-working AI solo company system into a weekly operating SOP covering content, website updates, leads, CRM, sales actions, and review.

## Core principle
The SOP should be simple enough that one person can actually follow it every week.

## Input checklist
Collect or infer when available:
- business type
- main offer
- current traffic channels
- content production ability
- website maintenance needs
- lead volume or expected lead volume
- CRM setup
- sales follow-up rhythm
- proposal or quote workflow
- main metrics currently tracked

## Default weekly rhythm
### Monday — Content and asset review
Check:
- homepage / service page / FAQ updates
- this week's traffic theme
- CTA or campaign focus

### Tuesday — Publishing
Do:
- publish one SEO / GEO article
or
- publish one short-video batch
or
- publish one customer education asset

### Wednesday — Lead and CRM review
Do:
- review new leads
- update CRM status
- send follow-up email / DM / booking invite

### Thursday — Sales and proposal work
Do:
- live consultations or demos
- proposal or quote sending
- objection handling
- sales next-step management

### Friday — Metrics and optimization review
Do:
- review traffic
- review leads and response speed
- review consultations, quotes, and conversions
- decide next week's top optimization priority

## SOP rules
- Adapt the routine to the student's actual business model.
- If the student has no traffic yet, replace publishing volume with launch preparation tasks.
- If the student has no CRM yet, include the minimum manual tracking step.
- Keep the routine operational, not theoretical.

## Suggested metrics
Examples:
- visits
- inquiries
- qualified leads
- follow-ups sent
- calls booked
- proposals sent
- closed deals

## 执行注意事项

## Guardrails
- Do not create a SOP that assumes a team if the operator is solo.
- Prefer consistency over high volume.
- Every weekday block should connect to revenue or pipeline health.
