# Class 11 Skills

This folder contains reusable skills and supporting classroom materials for **Class 11 — AI market research, product selection, and marketing intelligence**.

## Current skills

### `class11-market-research-agent`
Student-editable workflow that turns a market brief plus pasted evidence into pain-point analysis, competitor gap analysis, opportunity generation, scoring, and a final recommended niche.

## Purpose

Use these assets for repeatable workflows such as:

- turning messy market evidence into structured analysis
- comparing competitors without depending on live scraping
- ranking product or service opportunities before building
- teaching students how to justify one niche recommendation with evidence
- preparing reusable outputs for later validation, marketing, and product planning work
