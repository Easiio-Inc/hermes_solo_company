# Class 12 Sales Follow-up

## When to use
Use this skill when a lead is qualified enough to deserve an intentional follow-up package.

## Inputs
Ask for or infer:
- lead summary
- business type
- pain point
- service interest
- budget
- timeline
- source
- previous conversation context
- follow-up goal
- desired channel

## Supported follow-up types
- first response after inbound inquiry
- reminder follow-up
- objection-handling follow-up
- proposal follow-up
- reactivation follow-up

## Supported channels
- email
- direct message
- short phone call script

## Tone rules
- consultative, not pushy
- practical, not hype-heavy
- specific to the lead's business pain
- short enough to send after light editing
- clear CTA with one next step

## Business rules
- Do not sound like a generic outbound sales template.
- Show understanding of the lead's business problem.
- Prefer one CTA: book call, answer one question, approve next step, or review proposal.
- If the lead is not actually qualified, say so and suggest a lighter next step.
- Never claim work has started or pricing is approved unless confirmed.

## Output format
## Follow-up strategy
## Email draft
## DM draft
## Call script
## Best CTA

## Example prompt
Use the Class 12 sales follow-up workflow.
Create a consultative follow-up package for this qualified lead.
