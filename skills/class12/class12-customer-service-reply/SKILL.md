# Class 12 Customer-Service Reply

## When to use
Use this skill when the inquiry is mainly asking for help, clarification, scope explanation, pricing orientation, or scheduling information.

## Inputs
Collect when available:
- name
- company
- source
- question or inquiry text
- service interest
- urgency
- relevant FAQ or business context

## Purpose
Convert one inquiry into:
- a clear answer
- a helpful short reply
- a soft CTA when appropriate
- escalation guidance if the question is custom or high-risk

## Business rules
- Be helpful before being salesy.
- Answer the actual question directly.
- Use a soft CTA, not a hard close, unless the inquiry clearly asks for a next step.
- If pricing is unknown, give a range or process explanation instead of inventing a number.
- If the question is high-risk, legal, custom, or unclear, recommend human follow-up.

## Output format
## Inquiry summary
## Direct answer
## Email reply draft
## DM reply draft
## Escalation note
## Best next action

## Example prompt
Use the Class 12 customer-service reply workflow.
Answer this inquiry clearly and prepare a short email and DM response.
