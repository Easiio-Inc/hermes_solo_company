# Class 12 Skills

This folder contains reusable skills and supporting classroom materials for **Class 12 — AI customer service and sales agent workflows**.

## Current skills

### `class12-customer-service-sales-orchestrator`
Student-editable entry workflow that turns one inquiry, website lead, or chatbot transcript into the correct customer-service or sales route.

### `class12-lead-qualification`
Lead-scoring workflow that evaluates fit, urgency, budget, timeline, and buying intent.

### `class12-customer-service-reply`
Helpful customer-service reply workflow for email and DM drafts when the inquiry needs clarification, FAQ help, pricing orientation, or scheduling support.

### `class12-sales-followup`
Consultative sales follow-up workflow for email, DM, and call-script drafts.

### `class12-crm-update-handoff`
CRM-ready handoff workflow that converts a lead conversation into a summary, stage update, follow-up task, and operator next action.

## Purpose

Use these assets for repeatable workflows such as:

- routing incoming leads into the right response path
- separating customer-service questions from sales opportunities
- scoring lead quality before quoting or proposing work
- drafting consultative follow-up instead of cold-template responses
- keeping CRM updates and follow-up tasks consistent across students and operators
